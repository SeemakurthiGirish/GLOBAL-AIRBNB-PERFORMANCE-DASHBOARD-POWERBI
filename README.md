# 🌍 Global Airbnb Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Airbnb's global performance across 10 major cities — covering listings growth, pricing, ratings, review behavior, seasonality, and host trust signals.

---

## 📌 Project Purpose

This project was built to explore how Airbnb has scaled globally over more than a decade, and to uncover patterns in customer review behavior, host verification trust, city-wise market share, and seasonal travel trends. The goal was to turn raw listings and reviews data into a clean, decision-ready dashboard that tells a story — not just displays numbers.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Power BI Desktop** — dashboard design, data modeling & visualization
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** — calculated measures (cumulative %, averages, ratios)
- **Power Query (M)** — data cleaning, transformation & shaping
- **Excel** — initial data inspection and preprocessing

---

## 📂 Data Source

Dataset sourced from **[Maven Analytics](https://www.mavenanalytics.io/)** — Global Airbnb Listings dataset, covering 10 international cities with listings, hosts, reviews, pricing, and rating information from 2008 onward.

---

## ✨ Features & Highlights

- **Global KPIs** — 2,79,712 listings | 10 cities | 1,82,024 hosts | 144 property types | 5,373K reviews
- **Listings Lifecycle (2008–2020)** — visual breakdown of Airbnb's Introduction → Growth → Maturity → Decline → Reinvention → COVID-19 phases
- **Market Share by City** — Paris, NYC & Sydney drive ~48% of total listings and reviews
- **Price Comparison** — Hotel Room vs Entire Place vs Private Room vs Shared Room, by city
- **Ratings Breakdown** — best & worst rated cities, with cleanliness and value-for-money flagged as weakest metrics
- **Review Frequency Analysis** — 86% of guests review only once; identifies outlier/anomaly reviewers
- **Seasonality Trends** — monthly review share by city, highlighting peak travel seasons
- **Host Trust & Verification** — identity verification and profile picture adoption across hosts
